#### ~$ whoami
Hi there! I'm a software developer at heart — I just love reading and writing code, preferably in vim and with Python. I see learning as a lifelong pursuit: right now I'm picking up Spanish and the Go programming language. Over the years I've gained experience in several languages — Python, Java, JavaScript, and most recently Go.

For the past few years I've been getting back into IT security more seriously: I conduct penetration tests and help harden Linux systems.

Open source is especially close to my heart. I prefer to work on Linux — openSUSE, specifically. Just for fun, I build my own RPM and DEB packages and publish them on the [openSUSE Build Service](https://build.opensuse.org/project/show/home:jloehel). You can find my projects and contributions on [GitHub](https://github.com/jloehel).

I'm a big fan of puzzles — when time allows, I play [CTF tournaments](https://ctftime.org/user/38262) and stay active on [Hack The Box](https://profile.hackthebox.com/profile/019c90b9-99b9-73d0-9888-e3fbd4a86eab). I also enjoy reading comics, watching movies and TV shows, and traveling a lot.
